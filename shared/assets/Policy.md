# CloudVerse Ecosystem Policies
*Last Updated: 01 October 2026*

Welcome to **CloudVerse**. By using our bots (Drive, Mega, Rclone), you agree to these simplified terms.

## 1. Terms of Service
- **Acceptable Use**: Personal, non-commercial use only. Illegal activities, harassment, or distributing malware are strictly prohibited.
- **Fair Usage**: To keep the service fast for everyone, admins may limit bandwidth or transfers for heavy users.
- **Account Access**: Access is a privilege and may be revoked without notice for violations.
- **No Warranty**: Service is provided "AS IS". We are not responsible for data loss or downtime. Always back up your own files.

## 2. Privacy Policy
- **Data Collection**: We collect only your Telegram ID/Name and temporary transfer metadata for authentication and processing. 
- **Data Storage**: Your files are *never* stored permanently on our servers. They are securely deleted immediately after transfer.
- **Third-Parties**: Your files go straight to your chosen cloud provider (e.g., Google Drive, Mega), subject to their privacy rules. We never sell your data.
- **Log Scrubbing**: System logs are automatically scrubbed of sensitive data (passwords, tokens) to protect your privacy.
- **Log Retention**: System logs are retained for **5 days**. Each restart produces a new timestamped log archive which is automatically backed up and then deleted after 5 days.

## 3. Copyright Policy
- **Zero Tolerance**: You must own or have legal rights to the files you transfer. Piracy is strictly forbidden.
- **Content Moderation**: We do not scan your private transfers. However, any account discovered to be facilitating piracy will be instantly terminated.
- **DMCA Takedowns**: Copyright owners can contact us via our Support Bot. We cooperate fully with legitimate takedown requests.
